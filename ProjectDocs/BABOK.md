## Business Analysis Body of Knowledge (BABOK) - EstatePlanning

### Introduction
This documentation outlines the business requirements and analysis for the KSF EstatePlanning module. It serves as the foundational document connecting stakeholder needs to technical implementation.

### Stakeholder Needs
1. **Primary Group**: Financial Institutions and Wealth Management Firms
   - Need automated calculation of estate tax implications
   - Requirement for beneficiary allocation modeling
   - Demand for compliance reporting with inheritance laws

2. **Secondary Group**: Legal Professionals
   - Need scenario-based estate planning simulations
   - Request for documentation generation for executor preparation
   - Need for integration with case management systems

3. **Third Party**: Insurance Companies
   - Need actuarial modeling of estate settlement scenarios
   - Requirement for beneficiary designation validation
   - Engagement with policy settlement workflows

### Solution Approach
The EstatePlanning module will provide business logic for:
- Tax calculation across different jurisdictions
- Beneficiary allocation strategies
- Compliance verification with inheritance laws
- Scenario modeling for different estate planning approaches

### Acceptance Criteria
- Input validation determines jurisdiction type
- Tax calculation follows jurisdiction-specific rules
- Output reports meet regulatory formatting requirements
- Scenario comparison provides clear cost-benefit analysis
- All calculations pass validation against reference implementations
